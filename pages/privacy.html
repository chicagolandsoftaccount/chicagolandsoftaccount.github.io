Privacy

Privacy at a glance

Short version: this site doesn't track you.

This is a static personal portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and collects no personal data. There are no forms, accounts, or trackers.

A few assets (web fonts and icons) load from third-party CDNs — Google Fonts, cdnjs, and jsDelivr. Like any web request, those providers may record standard server-log information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. Nothing on this site reads or stores that information.

If you choose to email me, you're sharing your email address voluntarily so I can reply. I won't add it to any list or share it.

Questions? Reach out via the contact options on the home page.
